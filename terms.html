2. Description of the service

The App allows Users to:

  • View and browse liked videos from YouTube (synchronization and local organization on the device);
  • View videos uploaded to their own YouTube channel;
  • Browse playlists synced from YouTube (list, details, and video metadata);
  • Organize videos locally in the playlist editor in the Create tab (e.g. reordering on the device);
  • Browse YouTube Charts — lists derived from official YouTube Charts playlists; metadata may be refreshed on a schedule via the Developer's servers and stored in Firebase; video playback opens in the YouTube app or browser.

In the current version, the App uses read-only YouTube API access (youtube.readonly OAuth scope): it synchronizes and displays your library but does not create, edit, or delete playlists or other content on your YouTube channel. The App acts as a client for YouTube Data API v3 and does not host or store video files. Chart lists are aggregated metadata only (not your private account data).
